# 🚨 Report Kit — @LvNumBot (doxxing/data-selling bot) ban karwao

**Target bot:** `@LvNumBot` — "NUMBER TO INFORMATION ⚡" (t.me/LvNumBot, ~12,169 monthly users)

**Problem:** Ye bot users se phone number / Telegram ID leta hai aur us insaan ki
personal info (naam, address, ID documents) offer karta hai — bina uski consent
ke. Ye doxxing hai aur illegally obtained (hacked/leaked) data ka sale hai.
India mein ye Aadhaar Act 2016, IT Act 2000 (S.66C/66D) aur DPDP Act 2023 ke
under criminal offence hai.

> Ek report se bot nahi girta — **10-20 alag logon ki reports** se girta hai.
> Isi liye ye kit bana hai: sabko bhejo, sab report karein. 🤝

---

## ✅ Step 1 — Telegram app ke andar report (2 minute, sabse pehle)

1. Bot kholo: **t.me/LvNumBot** → **Start Bot**
2. Bot ke messages ko **select karo** (jo "number se info" claim karte hain)
3. Upar **⋮ (three-dot menu)** → **Report**
4. Reason chuno: **"Scam or Fraud"** (agar option ho to "Illegal Content" bhi theek hai)
5. Submit ✅

> Har report count hoti hai. Doston se bhi karwao — 5 minute ka kaam.

## ✅ Step 2 — @notoscam ko forward karo (2 minute)

1. **@notoscam** kholo (Telegram ki official anti-scam team)
2. Bot ke screenshots/claims **forward** karo + likho:
   `@LvNumBot is selling personal data (Aadhaar/PAN lookups) via phone numbers — doxxing bot, please review`

## ✅ Step 3 — abuse@telegram.org ko email (formal, sabse effective)

**To:** `abuse@telegram.org`
**Subject:** neeche wala copy karo
**Body:** neeche wala copy karo, screenshots attach karo (Step 7 dekho)

```
Subject: Doxxing bot @LvNumBot selling personal identity data (Aadhaar/PAN lookups)

Hello Telegram Abuse Team,

I would like to report the bot @LvNumBot (https://t.me/LvNumBot, display
name "NUMBER TO INFORMATION", ~12,000 monthly users).

What it does:
- Accepts a phone number or Telegram ID from any user
- In exchange, offers the target person's private information: name,
  address, and identity documents (Aadhaar, PAN) of Indian citizens
- Monetizes this by charging users for "full reports"

Why this violates Telegram's Terms of Service:
- Doxxing: disclosing individuals' personal data without their knowledge
  or consent
- Facilitating illegal activity: in India, obtaining/trading such
  identity data violates the Aadhaar Act 2016, the Information
  Technology Act 2000 (S.66C/66D), and the Digital Personal Data
  Protection Act 2023. The bot primarily targets Indian users.
- Selling access to what appears to be stolen/leaked databases

Request: please terminate this bot and review its associated payment
channels and administrator accounts.

Evidence attached: screenshots of the bot's claims and payment demands.

Thank you.
```

## ✅ Step 4 — India: cybercrime.gov.in par complaint (5-10 minute)

1. **cybercrime.gov.in** kholo
2. **"Report Other Cybercrime"** chuno (ye financial fraud nahi hai, isliye
   "other" category)
3. Category: **Crime against women/child nahi** → "Other cybercrime"
   → data theft / privacy violation jaisi category chuno
4. Description box mein neeche wala paste karo:

```
I want to report a Telegram bot that is illegally selling Indian
citizens' personal data, including Aadhaar and PAN details.

Bot details:
- Platform: Telegram
- Bot username: @LvNumBot
- Link: https://t.me/LvNumBot
- Display name: "NUMBER TO INFORMATION"
- Approx. users: 12,000+ monthly

The bot invites users to submit any person's phone number or Telegram
ID and offers to reveal that person's private information (name,
address, Aadhaar/PAN card details) for payment. This is doxxing and
trading of illegally obtained personal data, which violates the
Aadhaar Act 2016, IT Act 2000 (S.66C/66D), and DPDP Act 2023.

I have attached screenshots of the bot's claims and payment demands.
Requesting investigation and takedown of this bot and its underlying
data source.
```

5. Screenshots upload karo, apna details bharo, submit
6. Complaint number note kar lo

> 💡 Agar kisi ne bot ko **paisa diya** hai (fraud) → turant **1930** helpline
> par call karo (golden hour — bank freeze ho sakta hai) + same portal par
> financial fraud complaint.

## ✅ Step 6 — (Optional) CERT-In ko data-breach info

Bada data leak dikh raha ho to **CERT-In** (India's cyber security agency) ko
bhi batao: **incident@cert-in.org.in** — same details bhej do (English mein,
short).

## 📸 Step 7 — Evidence checklist (report se pehle collect karo)

| # | Kya capture karna hai | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bot ka full profile — naam, @username, description | Identity confirm |
| 2 | /start ke baad bot ke messages — "number se info" claims | Core violation proof |
| 3 | Payment demand — UPI ID / QR / "unlock ₹XXX" | Monetization proof |
| 4 | Koi sample "report" jo masked data dikhaye (agar free mein dikhe) | Method proof |
| 5 | Date/time visible (phone ka clock/status bar) | Timestamp |
| 6 | t.me/LvNumBot ka browser screenshot + "12K monthly users" | Scale proof |

**⚠️ Kya NAHI karna:**
- ❌ Payment mat karo (paisa doob jayega + criminal liability tumhari)
- ❌ Apna asli number/ID documents bot ko mat do
- ❌ Bot ke admin ko harass mat karo — sirf report karo
- ❌ Kisi aur ka number "test" ke liye mat daalo (wahi crime ho jayega)

## 🤝 Step 8 — Mass reporting (asli takedown isi se hota hai)

3. 20-50 reports ke baad Telegram review fast-track karta hai
4. #StaySafeOnline jaise communities/NGOs ko bhi tag kar sakte ho
